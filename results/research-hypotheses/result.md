# Historical research proposals remain hypotheses rather than measured HE4 improvements

## Outcome

The archived reviews cover optimizer/sign dynamics, learned quantizers, curricula, smaller block models, coordinate changes, low-rank corrections, vocabulary support and cost-aware draft policies. Their source/literature arguments are useful design inputs; they are not a tested unified recipe or a current backlog authorization.

## Scope and limitations

September/October source and literature reviews, often for older EAGLE or DSpark graphs. Publication claims were not freshly rechecked during this archive adoption. Current geometry, gradients, serialization and native contracts must be assessed before reuse.

## Decision

Retain the idea map with provenance; leave scientific choices unresolved.
