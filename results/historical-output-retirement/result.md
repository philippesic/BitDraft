# Historical measurements replace raw archives and closed qualification outputs

The remaining historical Windows archives and closed qualification runs are
retired after extracting and verifying compact evidence. Keep large files only when
they support current development. Earlier directory removal had preserved these raw
bytes wholesale and therefore had not completed their scientific closeout.

## Outcome

The Windows conversion authenticated all 155,279 indexed members across 422 archives.
The nested-transfer inspection examined 2,769 containers and another 181,858 members.
The combined catalog covers 1,175 source-path groups and 1,809 distinct report records
containing 89,368 numerical or status fields, preserving incomplete, adverse and failed
outcomes. Different contents saved under the same historical pathname remain separate
versions. All 116 matching request-performance reports contribute to the
[historical observations](windows-observations.json); no favorable-run selection or
pooling across workloads was performed. [Folder coverage](windows-families.csv) and
the compact support index preserve the original names and byte identities.

The qualification conversion records [229 result/arm entries](qualification.csv),
[16 campaign states](qualification-campaigns.json) and 18 failure records. Request
counters, timings and live-task totals were independently recomputed for 97 measured
arms and matched their source summaries. All original report rows remain available
in compact support, including the rows needed for grouped statistics.

The completed retirement reduced the archive/qualification footprint by **274.01 GiB**.
Linux had 327.90 GiB available afterward. These are allocated Linux filesystem bytes;
no Windows virtual-disk compaction was performed.

[Exact accounting and artifact identities](result.json) distinguish original raw-output
retirement from removal of temporary extraction staging. All plans completed through
the maintained retirement owners; every planned file is absent except small records
explicitly restored for subsequent development. The 137,898 temporary extraction blobs
were removed after their compact support archives passed readback authentication.

Both current TRAIN corpora passed admission through their maintained readers: 6,000 new
records and 15,647 original records. Payload loading passed for each of the three new-corpus
backing folders, an original record and a diagnostic DEV record. The diagnostic recipe
retains 120 fixed windows over 32 DEV tasks. All 10 current cached comparison definitions
authenticated. The complete saved-reference audit authenticated 52 records and 2,960
backing-file occurrences.

## Scope and limitations

This is historical evidence conversion and storage retirement. It performs no training,
capture, model allocation or new GPU benchmark. Existing scientific conclusions remain
in [FINDINGS](../../FINDINGS.md), including their original data roles, hardware and timing
definitions. Imported producer verdicts are historical records rather than new
certification. Earlier heldout observations remain consumed evidence and never enter
training or selection.

Every archive member was checked against its existing SHA-256 and byte length while
streaming the archives. Preserved report, configuration, diagnostic and source bytes
were read back from compact support and rehashed. This verifies preservation, not the
correctness of every historical experiment. Some archived runs lack sufficient provenance
for exact reproduction; their recorded source identities and limitations are preserved.

## Artifact disposition

Retain verified compact measurements, diagnostics, exact executed source/configuration
where available, historical content identities and deletion receipts. Retain the current
development dependencies listed above. Retire the compressed Windows raw-output archives,
closed qualification optimizer states, unused exports, redundant capture fixtures and
raw profiler/event outputs through their maintained owners.

Exact old optimizer resumes, discarded raw capture tensors, profiler timelines and
runtime/event replay become unavailable. Recorded measurements and source survive;
they do not replace discarded weights or inputs for exact reruns. Current corpora and
the original continuation parent remain intact. Final executed disposition is recorded
in the authenticated retirement receipts and final accounting.

The audit of earlier compact archives found a shortlisted model and feature tensors
embedded in two supposedly metadata/source archives. Those are also retired after
preserving their useful report/source contents. Independent Git history and native WIP
recovery archives remain; small upstream vocabulary fixtures inside a native WIP snapshot
are retained as part of that exact source recovery, rather than as trained candidates.
