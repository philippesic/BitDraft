# Compact evidence replaces obsolete legacy runs while current training inputs survive

## Outcome

The authorized November 12 closeout verified compact historical evidence and retired
16,881 large legacy files:202.54 GiB of unique physical data inside the old checkout.
Three additional obsolete Windows optimizer archives,11.87 GiB, were retired separately.
These figures exclude further space released by retiring34 inactive linked checkouts
and their duplicate Git/native metadata. Windows host reclaim and WSL logical free space
are different measurements; no virtual-disk compaction was performed.

The compact support keeps 57,346 report/measurement/table paths and 86,805 source/config
paths in a deduplicated63 MiB archive, plus a compressed path index. Native source/header/
build snapshots and external-checkout records have separate authenticated archives.
All selected comparison arms and request counters were included regardless of outcome.
230 historical training streams have descriptive 256-row numeric aggregates; 16,233 logs
were examined for failure excerpts. Large summary objects were preserved separately.

All registered project and native HEADs are independently recoverable. Both dirty native
checkouts were reconstructed from the saved staged/unstaged patches and untracked files;
Git status and both diffs matched exactly, including 2,070 untracked native files.
The four provenance locks were retired only after their source versions were archived.
Historical absolute source paths are locators; their bytes now recover independently.

## Scope and limitations

This is evidence preservation and authorized retirement, not a new acceptance, performance
or model-quality result. Existing findings remain scoped to their original experiments.
Compact request measurements support their recorded counters/timings. Aggregated training
curves cannot reconstruct per-example updates or new uncertainty intervals. Text diagnostic
matches do not independently classify scientific failures.

Old B0, foundation, HE4, Csmallbroad and its accepted continuation full optimizer archives
are deliberately unavailable. The same applies to discarded feature/logit tensors,
profiles, process/event streams and frozen environments. Exact old optimization resumes
and raw tensor/event replay cannot be recovered from a chart, hash or source archive.
The retained original 220095 HE4broad parent is a different state and remains intact.

All preservation is local. Independent Git readers and archive extraction were verified;
this does not constitute an off-machine backup or a new GPU reproduction claim.

## Decision

Discard obsolete historical implementation outputs after their useful measurements,
findings and executed source have been preserved and checked. Keep current model/data
inputs and needed recovery/selected state with their dependency closure. At future task
closeout, decide disposition explicitly rather than keeping every artifact indefinitely.
The project check rejects result pages without artifact disposition. The maintained
retirement command verifies an exact authorized plan and preserves a deletion journal.

## Evidence and reproduction

[Authenticated receipt and support catalog](result.json) identifies compact archives,
source snapshots, exact deletion plans, journals, restoration checks and relocation maps.
[Evidence access](../../docs/evidence.md#compact-legacy-artifact-evidence) explains selective
recovery. Existing [findings](../../FINDINGS.md) remain the entrance to scientific lessons.
The former five-state registry is preserved in the retirement receipt instead of an
obsolete active policy file.

The first disposal attempt stopped on one owned read-only runtime directory after 28
files. The failure and permission repair are preserved. The same plan and journal then
completed, and every planned removal was checked absent. A regression test exercises
journaled filesystem failure and exact recovery. No unrelated job was stopped.
