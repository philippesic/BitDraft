# Five protected training states survived the November 6 cleanup

## Outcome

34 optimizer archives were deliberately retired, and five protected full states survived.
HE4, Csmallbroad, B0, foundation and the accepted Csmallbroad continuation remain
separate identities. Their compression bases, dictionaries, source and capture closure
must be preserved.

## Scope and limitations

the retirement receipt overrides older preservation statements for the exact
retired paths. Current metadata is not a fresh multi-GiB hash or an independent backup.

## Availability correction: November12,2026

The later authorized [compact-evidence closeout](../legacy-artifact-closeout/result.md)
retired these five obsolete full optimizer archives after preserving their useful
measurements and source. Exact resumes of these states are no longer available. The
original220095 HE4broad parent and current training corpora are separate and remain intact.
The November6 survival counts above are historical observations, not current availability.

## Decision

Retain this scoped finding for future decisions. No new experimental or deployment decision is made by this documentation migration.
