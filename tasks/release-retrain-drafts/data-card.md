# Data card (draft)

Draft for the release retrain ([task](../release-retrain.md)). Bracketed items are filled
after training and the gate. Counts are from `bank-v2` and the production order.

## Summary

- 100,000 TRAIN prompts, drawn from 14 of the 15 audited sources (bigcode/commitpackft contributes
  no TRAIN prompt)
  (`benchmarks/definitions/retrain-sources-v1.json`); one pass in a category- and
  length-balanced order (`order_seed` 9401, 100 stages of 1,000 prompts).
- Every supervised response is written by the target, Qwen3.5-4B Q4_K_M, in the project's
  runtime: thinking on and off, greedy and sampled. Training labels are the target's own
  choices on replay. No response text from any source dataset is trained on.
- Earlier turns kept as context: OpenAssistant human turns (Apache-2.0) and SWE-rebench agent
  histories (CC BY 4.0, generated by Qwen3-Coder-480B, Apache-2.0); only the next assistant
  turn is supervised.
- Generated on an RTX 5090 [stages written: all 100 / or the list]; features replayed on that
  GPU. The 4-bit control replays the same token streams on an RTX 5080.

## Exclusions and decontamination

- Excluded sources and reasons are listed in `retrain-sources-v1.json` (MATH and AoPS-derived
  sets, the Nemotron math split, share-alike and closed-model-generated sets, unaudited legacy
  prompts). Salesforce xLAM is pending and unused.
- Protected material: heldout24, the development, final and sealed confirmation indexes and the
  public gate never enter TRAIN. Admission matches IDs, groups, source keys, aliases and SHA-256
  of each user text (raw and whitespace-normalised). `bank-v2` replaced exactly six `bank-v1`
  GSM8K rows whose text matched protected fingerprints (one GSM8K and five Orca-Math rows were
  added); a final scan found 0 matches against all four indexes (positive control: 6 in
  `bank-v1`). Provenance: `artifacts/bank/retrain-01/provenance/bank-v2-provenance-supplement.json`.
- Public gate: 60 SWE-rebench repositories are reserved for the gate and never enter TRAIN;
  gate items and repositories are listed in the gate's exclusion file.

## Known limitations

- Multi-turn sources mostly contribute their first user turn.
- Responses inherit the target's behaviour, including its errors; they are not checked for
  correctness.
- Orca-Math questions are partly model-generated (GPT-4 family); recorded as a low residual
  terms risk.
- Candidate and 4-bit control train on features replayed on different GPUs (5090 and 5080);
  measured cross-GPU label differences 1.67%, no material acceptance difference.
